The users are saying that the rules explaination in the, how to play isn't clear. Use this it don't have to be specifically the same but it must follow this procedure.

a. Board Set Up
A large 3×3 grid, and inside each of the 9 big squares, draw a standard smaller 3×3 tic-tac-toe board.

b. Follow the Move Rule
The first player place in any empty spot on any of the 9 small boards. Your specific cell choice dictates where your opponent must play next: whatever relative position you pick inside your small board forces your opponent to play inside the corresponding small board on the main grid (for example, playing in the bottom-right corner sends them to the bottom-right small board). If you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you get a "free turn" and can play on any open board.

c. Claim Small Boards to Win the Game

Win a small board by getting three-in-a-row just like regular tic-tac-toe, which conquers that entire big square for your symbol. The first player to get three small boards in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on the large global board wins the game
