Three rules, one twist

If you can play regular tic-tac-toe, you already know 90% of this. Here's the part that changes everything.

Place in any square

Each of the 9 small boards works like normal tic-tac-toe — claim squares to build a line of three.

Your move sends them

Wherever you play inside a board, your opponent must play in the matching board next — position 3 sends them to board 3.

Win boards to win the game

If that board is already won or full, they choose freely. Take three local boards in a row to win it all.
