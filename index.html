How to play

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe is regular tic-tac-toe played on nine boards at once. Learn three rules and you're ready to play.

  1. 1

    Board set up

    The game is played on a large 3×3 grid. Inside each of its 9 big squares is a standard, smaller 3×3 tic-tac-toe board.

    That makes 9 small boards and 81 cells in total. X always goes first.

  2. 2

    Follow the move rule

    The first player may place in any empty cell on any of the 9 small boards.

    After that, your cell choice decides where your opponent must play next. Whichever position you pick inside your small board is the position of the small board your opponent has to play in. Play the bottom-right corner, and they must play in the bottom-right small board.

    Free turn: if you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you get a free turn and can play on any open board.

    In the game, the glowing board is the one you must play in.

  3. 3

    Claim small boards to win the game

    Win a small board by getting three in a row, just like regular tic-tac-toe. That conquers the whole big square for your symbol.

    The first player to claim three small boards in a row on the large board, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, wins the game.

    A small board that fills up with no winner counts for nobody. If every board is finished and no one has a line, the game is a draw.
